# Practitioner-to-Practitioner Medical Negligence LMS

## About the Project

The Practitioner-to-Practitioner Medical Negligence LMS is a professional learning platform designed for **medical negligence attorneys, advocates and medical experts**.

The platform will support practitioner-to-practitioner training through:

- Structured legal and medical literature
- Case law and legislation
- Video-based learning
- Practical case studies
- Interactive simulations
- Training games
- Assessments and quizzes
- Peer-to-peer discussion
- Facilitator-led training sessions
- Progress tracking

## Vision

To create an interactive professional learning environment where legal practitioners and medical experts can learn from one another through realistic medical negligence litigation scenarios.

The platform is designed to move professional training beyond passive reading and toward practical, collaborative learning.

## Learning Approach

The core learning cycle is:

**Learn → Watch → Analyse → Practise → Play → Discuss → Assess**

## Proposed Learning Structure

Each training module will follow a common structure:

1. Learning objectives
2. Required reading
3. Additional literature
4. Video lesson
5. Worked example
6. Case scenario
7. Interactive activity
8. Practitioner discussion
9. Assessment
10. Reflection
11. Facilitator guidance

## Initial Curriculum

The initial curriculum is expected to include:

### Module 1 — Foundations of Medical Negligence Litigation

Introduction to the legal, medical and evidentiary framework.

### Module 2 — Medical Records and Chronology

Understanding medical records, constructing chronologies and identifying evidentiary gaps.

### Module 3 — Expert Evidence

Selecting, briefing and working with medical experts and analysing opposing expert evidence.

### Module 4 — Causation

Exploring clinical causation, legal causation and competing expert opinions.

### Module 5 — Quantum and Damages

Understanding evidence required to establish damages and the role of different experts.

### Module 6 — Trial Preparation and Advocacy

Preparing witnesses, presenting evidence and conducting examination and cross-examination.

## Content Governance

Because the platform deals with professional legal and medical education, learning materials should be carefully managed.

Each published resource should identify, where applicable:

- Author or creator
- Source
- Jurisdiction
- Publication date
- Version or effective date
- Copyright or licence status
- Reviewer
- Review date

Confidential client information, identifiable patient information and privileged legal material must not be uploaded to the repository.
