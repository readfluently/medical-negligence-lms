The Client Walks Into Your Office

First Consultation and Initial Investigation

About This Book

This book begins at the point where the potential client walks into your office.

The task is not to decide immediately whether the practitioner was negligent. The task is to investigate the story, establish the facts, identify what remains unknown, and build the foundation for the legal analysis that follows.

Investigate the story before deciding the case.
