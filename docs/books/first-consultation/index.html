First Consultation and Initial Investigation

The Starting Point

Before You Decide There Is a Case

A medical negligence matter does not begin with an expert report, a summons, or an allegation that a practitioner was negligent.

It begins with a person walking into your office and telling you what happened.

At this stage, the task is not to decide the case. The task is to investigate the story.

You must establish what happened, when it happened, who was involved, what the patient's condition was before treatment, what changed afterwards, what records exist, and what remains unknown.

Practitioner’s Principle

Investigate the story before deciding the case.

The Objective of Book 1

By the end of this stage, you should not necessarily have decided whether the practitioner was negligent.

You should have something more valuable: a structured understanding of the story, the evidence, the unanswered questions, and the issues that must be investigated next.
