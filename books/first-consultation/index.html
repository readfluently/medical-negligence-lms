The Client Walks Into Your Office

A Practical Guide to the First Medical Negligence Consultation

← Back to Practitioner Library

Every medical-negligence matter begins with a story.

A patient or family member walks into the practitioner's office and explains what happened. The account may be incomplete. The medical records may not yet be available. Dates may be uncertain. The client may already believe that negligence occurred.

The practitioner's first task is not to decide the case.

It is to investigate the story, establish the facts, identify what is known and unknown, and begin building the case map.

The organising principle of Book 1:

Listen to the story. Ask the questions. Obtain the records. Build the chronology. Identify the medical event. Identify the injury. Record the unanswered questions.

Do not decide the case before investigating it.
